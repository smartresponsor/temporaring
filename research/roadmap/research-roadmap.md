# System Tempo research roadmap

The roadmap is conservative: advancement means surviving stronger falsification and
operationalization requirements.

- **v0.1:** temporal-reparameterization classification and reproducible symbolic tests.
- **v0.2:** coupled dynamical systems, relative tempo beyond the v0.1 active-factor compatibility filter, global/local `tau` obstructions, and explicit clock/reference subsystems.
- **v0.3:** thermodynamic systems; distinguish tempo effects from reparameterized kinetics.
- **v0.4:** field/PDE extensions only after finite-dimensional criteria are mature.
- **v0.5:** quantum models only after the role of time and observables is stated precisely.
- **v0.6:** cosmology/observational constraints only after a defensible invariant formalism
  exists.

Version labels may change; the conservative progression should not.
