# Open research questions

These questions are intentionally unresolved. They define the research backlog rather than claims already established by Temporaring.

## Reparameterization and invariance

- When does a local time transformation fail to extend globally?
- Which singularities in `Gamma` are coordinate failures, and which change physical structure?
- How should zeros and sign changes of `Gamma` be classified?
- Which phase-space properties remain invariant under admissible transformations?

## Relative tempo

- Can `R_AB = Gamma_A / Gamma_B` be defined operationally for interacting subsystems?
- What minimal coupling makes relative tempo observable rather than conventional?
- Can one global `tau` remove two subsystem tempo factors while preserving coupling?
- Which dimensionless ratios, phases, event counts, or synchronization observables are decisive?
- Does physical novelty require at least two independently modelled clocks or reference processes?

## Model boundaries

- How should explicitly non-autonomous forcing transform?
- Which finite-dimensional conclusions survive extension to PDEs or field models?
- What changes when stochastic terms are introduced?
- What additional invariance criteria are required before quantum, relativistic, or cosmological use?

## Operational meaning

- What exactly constitutes a clock in a System Tempo model?
- Which observable comparison distinguishes tempo dynamics from ordinary rate variation?
- Can the framework produce a dimensionless prediction different from a baseline theory?

## Prioritization rule

Prefer the cheapest decisive falsification. An inexpensive counterexample outranks a large simulation that cannot distinguish parameterization from physics.
